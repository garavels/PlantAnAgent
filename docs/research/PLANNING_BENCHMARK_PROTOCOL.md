# Planning benchmark protocol

Date: **2026-09-02**

Status: **pre-outcome development protocol; no relaxed-bound or future-information
planner outcome has been generated**

## Purpose

The first causal-controller study showed that a stateless deadline rule can be much worse
than a simple persistent operating rule. It did not answer either of the questions needed
before RL:

1. how much delivery is physically attainable in the current task; or
2. whether the existing five-field action contract can express a materially better exact
   trajectory.

This protocol creates a two-sided controllability bracket before a deployable MPC is
built:

```text
legacy exact-simulator trajectory
    <= best-known feasible future-information trajectory
    <= dual-certified continuous-relaxation delivery bound
```

The left inequality is enforced by retaining the complete legacy trajectory as an
incumbent. The right inequality applies to delivered methane only and follows from an
explicit feasible-set relaxation. Neither inequality says that the middle planner is
optimal. The upper bound is not a controller and cannot be compared as if it shared the
online information set.

## Prior-outcome and split boundary

Rates 35 and 42 kg/h, seeds 0--2, 72-hour episodes, the current synthetic weather regime,
and the commitment-alpha equipment profile are development-contaminated. Seed 3 is also
contaminated by an earlier smoke run. They may be used to implement and debug this
benchmark, but no result from them may be relabelled prospective, held out, or commercial.

No uninspected seed or regime will be opened during implementation. A later prospective
amendment must freeze the code revision, solver version, exact seed list, regimes, sample
size, endpoints, margins, and failure rules before those outcomes are generated.

## Questions and gates

### P1 — Relaxed physical headroom

What is the largest cumulative methane delivery admitted by a minute-resolution linear
relaxation with perfect solar truth and the exact commitment schedule?

- If the certified bound is within numerical tolerance of A0, the cell is saturated for
  delivery and should be made more demanding before RL tuning.
- If the bound is materially above A0, physical/resource headroom exists, but action-space
  attainability is still unknown.

### P2 — Exact action-space headroom

Can a replay-verified action schedule through the exact guarded simulator outperform the
legacy incumbent when selected with privileged future information?

- If no, improve the fixed search or reconsider the action interface; do not infer that
  no better trajectory exists.
- If yes, a realizable scheduling gap exists and a causal optimizer is justified.

### P3 — Causal capture

Can deterministic receding-horizon control using only the current public forecast capture
a useful fraction of the feasible future-information gain without worsening safety,
terminal balances, or cycling?

P3 is a later protocol amendment. No implementation may be called MPC until it solves a
declared multi-period optimization model and reports solver status. The current environment
has no calibrated costs or prices, so the first implementation will be called **service
MPC**, not economic MPC.

## Part I — Dual-certified delivery relaxation

### Privileged input and isolation

The relaxation receives a private, immutable `EpisodeV2`, `EpisodeConfigV2`,
`PlantConfig`, active `Constraints`, and the full checkpoint-contract identity. It may read
the complete solar truth tape, delivery schedule, and active electrolyser commitment
profile. It does not implement `ControllerV2`, cannot enter `run_controller`, and never
contributes to a causal-controller contrast. This first implementation is deliberately
scoped to the fault-free Environment V2 episodes generated by `sample_episode`. It fails
closed if the scenario contains any scheduled fault, an initial state field is absent,
initial battery energy is below the active reserve, or the active stack configuration and
commitment profile cannot establish the best-case yield used by the relaxation. Faulted
episodes require a later relaxation with explicit capacity-loss and availability
semantics; they are not silently analysed with this model.

### Resolution and variables

One optimization interval equals the one-minute simulator physics interval. For every
interval `t`, the continuous variables are:

- solar power to battery, electrolyser, DAC, and methanator;
- battery power to the electrolyser;
- curtailed solar power;
- H2 production, CO2 capture, and CH4 production;
- boundary battery energy;
- boundary H2, CO2, and CH4 inventories; and
- methane delivered at every declared deadline.

All power is bus-side kW, stored energy is kWh, mass is kg, and variables are
non-negative. Every variable also has an explicit, finite independent upper bound. Solar
routes and curtailment are bounded by that interval's available PV; battery support by
the active support and discharge limits; production by the relevant healthy component
cap, timestep, and best nominal yield; boundary states by configured storage/capacity;
and deliveries by their individual requests. These boxes are part of the serialized
model and are required by the dual-certificate construction below.

### Retained constraints

The linear program retains:

- exact private solar truth and per-minute solar-bus balance. For episode interval `t`,
  the fixed PV value is exactly
  `clear_sky_kw(start_s + t * dt_s) * weather.tape.for_interval(t)`, equivalently the
  clear-sky value times `boundary_factors[t + 1]`; the resulting vector is hashed and
  checked interval by interval against a simulator replay, including sunrise and day
  wrap;
- component nameplate and active safety caps;
- battery charge/discharge power, capacity, active reserve, and both efficiencies;
- the architectural restriction that battery discharge can feed only the electrolyser;
- H2, CO2, and CH4 storage capacities;
- within-minute storage headroom in the simulator's physical order:
  `H2[t] + H2_produced[t] <= H2_capacity`,
  `CO2[t] + CO2_captured[t] <= CO2_capacity`, and
  `CH4[t] + CH4_produced[t] <= CH4_capacity`, before same-minute methanation and
  settlement withdrawals;
- configured H2 and CO2 requirements per kg CH4;
- configured DAC and methanator specific energy;
- upper-yield inequalities for H2, CO2, and CH4 production from accepted power,
  including a best-case electrolyser specific energy derived from the active plant
  configuration and commitment profile (52 kWh/kg H2 for the current profile);
- production-before-settlement ordering in a deadline's final minute;
- each commitment's amount and exact due step; and
- all material and energy recurrences from the sampled initial state.

The primary objective maximizes cumulative methane delivered. Results report requested
mass, the relaxed fill ceiling, and the certified delivery bound. Every settlement,
terminal inventory, terminal battery energy, component-energy allocation, and curtailment
value is labelled as one possibly non-unique numerical primal witness; none is implied to
be individually optimal or certified. Balance residuals are independently recomputed from
that witness.

### Deliberate relaxations

The LP removes or improves only constraints that can reduce exact-simulator production:

- no cold/hot startup, cooldown, hot-standby consumption, minimum load, minimum up/down,
  shortfall grace, or ramp restriction;
- no part-load penalty; the best nominal electrolyser efficiency applies at every load;
- simultaneous battery charge and discharge are permitted by the mathematical model;
- share-action and dispatch-priority restrictions are replaced by direct continuous power
  routing.

These choices can make the LP optimistic, never more restrictive than a valid exact
trajectory inside the declared fault-free scope. Every such exact trajectory embeds by
assigning its realized solar routes, battery support, inventories, production, and
deliveries to the LP variables. Production is bounded above by nominal power yield rather
than forced equal to it; this is necessary to embed startup, standby, part-load loss, and
refusal intervals that accept energy without the nominal output. The derived coefficient
must be no larger than the best attainable specific energy under the active profile; that
proof and the profile digest are serialized. All removed commitment/share constraints only
enlarge the LP feasible set. The initial-reserve precondition prevents the LP's reserve
floor from excluding an otherwise legal initial boundary. Therefore the LP optimum is an
upper bound on cumulative delivered methane under the retained configuration.

The proof obligation is part of the tests: exact trace quantities must satisfy the relaxed
balances within tolerance, and sampled exact deliveries must never exceed the bound.

### Endowment-preserving diagnostic

A second solve may require terminal H2, CO2, CH4, and battery energy to be no lower than
their initial values. It answers whether full service is compatible with restoring the
randomized starting endowment in the relaxed model. It is a useful anti-liquidation
diagnostic, but it is a restriction rather than a relaxation and **must not be called an
upper bound on the current task objective**.

### Solver and certification

The implementation will use the locked research dependency
`scipy.optimize.linprog(method="highs")` with sparse constraints. SciPy documents the
HiGHS methods as its current fast linear-programming implementations and exposes explicit
success/status, objective, residual, and iteration information
([SciPy `linprog`](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.optimize.linprog.html)).

HiGHS' primal objective alone is not used as an upper-bound certificate: for a maximization
LP, a merely feasible primal value lies on the wrong side of the optimum. The serialized
minimization form is `min cᵀx` subject to `A_ub x <= b_ub`, `A_eq x = b_eq`, and finite
boxes `l <= x <= u`. SciPy's inequality-marginal convention is frozen as `y_ub <= 0`.
Returned inequality marginals are projected with `min(y_ub, 0)` and equality marginals
remain free. With

```text
q = c - A_ubᵀ y_ub - A_eqᵀ y_eq
L = b_ubᵀ y_ub + b_eqᵀ y_eq
    + sum_i min(q_i * l_i, q_i * u_i),
```

weak duality makes `L` a lower bound on the minimization objective for any projected
multipliers; stationarity need not be assumed. The implementation evaluates this expression
independently with exact rational arithmetic over the stored IEEE-754 model coefficients
and projected returned multipliers. Conversion of `-L` to a binary-float delivery upper
bound is directed toward positive infinity and checked against the exact rational value.
Requested methane is likewise summed exactly and converted upward before it can cap the
bound. This removes an undocumented floating-point “margin” from the proof path. The
reported exact-simulator ceiling is the smaller of the upward-rounded requested mass and
the dual-certified bound; the solver's numerical primal value and primal--dual gap are
reported separately. Lower/upper-bound marginals are not used because the box minimization
handles those constraints directly. The magnitude of any inequality-marginal projection
is retained, and projection beyond the frozen tolerance prevents a numerically-tight
label even though weak duality still makes the resulting bound valid.

An artifact may publish `dual_certified_delivery_upper_bound_kg` only when all of the
following hold:

- the solver returns `success=True` and status `0`;
- every returned variable is finite;
- primal equality, inequality, variable-bound, energy, and mass residuals pass the frozen
  numerical tolerance;
- the independently recomputed exact-rational dual bound is finite, no smaller than the
  solver's numerical primal delivery, and within the declared optimality tolerance;
- delivered mass does not exceed requested mass; and
- the model, solver, source, dependency lock, episode, and result identities are serialized.

Iteration/time limits, infeasibility, unboundedness, numerical difficulty, missing solver
support, or validation failure are retained failures. The solver may be described as
numerically optimal, but that phrase is kept distinct from the dual-certified upper-bound
field. No numeric ceiling is published from a failed solve.

## Part II — Perfect-information best-known feasible planner

### Scope and naming

This is a deterministic offline search over deep copies of `PlantEnvV2`. It is allowed to
select a schedule after inspecting the complete episode. It is therefore privileged and
non-deployable. It will be called **best-known feasible future-information planner**, not
oracle optimum, MPC, controller upper bound, or proof of optimality.

It must remain a separate type from `ControllerV2`; in particular it may receive an
environment/episode, whereas online controllers may not.

### Feasibility and incumbent guarantee

Every candidate advances the exact one-minute simulator through the normal
`step_action` path, including the guard, five-field action, commitment state machine,
storage ordering, delivery settlement, and reward/accounting code. The winning schedule
is replayed from a fresh reset; only the fresh replay metrics are evidence.

The complete A0 action schedule is always evaluated and retained as the incumbent. The
planner returns the candidate with the greatest frozen final score, so it cannot report a
schedule worse than A0 under that score. Exact metric equality between search state and
fresh replay is required.

### Search freeze before outcomes

Before any full development result is opened, an amendment must freeze:

- action-library names, formulas, ordering, and digest;
- search block size, beam width, deduplication bins, and expansion budget;
- deterministic tie-breaking and random-seed policy;
- intermediate ranking and final lexicographic score;
- the incumbent comparison rule;
- runtime/memory limits and failure behavior; and
- the exact serialized search/replay schema.

The fixed action library will cover at least idle, charge, explicit warm retention,
commit/start support, several stack loads with stoichiometric DAC/methanator routing,
feedstock recovery, methane conversion, and the live legacy action. Candidate generators
may use private truth only in this privileged planner and must serialize the actual
five-field action chosen at each decision.

The final score will prioritize, in order:

1. cumulative delivered methane;
2. commitment service level;
3. worst commitment fill;
4. terminal methane plus CH4-equivalent feedstock potential,
   `CH4 + min(H2 / h2_per_ch4, CO2 / co2_per_ch4)`; this deliberately ignores the
   time and energy still needed for conversion and is only a tie-breaker, not “deliverable
   methane”;
5. terminal battery energy;
6. fewer forced/aborted/cold starts;
7. lower curtailment and battery throughput, where throughput is the exact-replay sum of
   absolute bus-side battery power times the physics timestep (charge plus discharge kWh).

No shaped scalar reward can outrank delivery. All comparisons use the same sampled
episode and exact manifest identity.

## Part III — Causal service MPC boundary

MPC is authorized only if Parts I and II demonstrate actionable headroom or reveal a
specific search/action limitation worth resolving. Its legal inputs are exactly:

- `ControllerObservationV2`, including current boundary state, preceding-interval
  aggregates, next announced delivery, current public forecast, and progress;
- static `PlantConfig`, `Constraints`, and commitment profile; and
- immutable constructor settings plus controller-local history.

It cannot receive `PlantEnvV2`, `PlantSimulator`, `EpisodeV2`, seed, replay hash, future
truth, future forecast vintages, or the private schedule. Information-isolation tests must
change private future truth without changing the current public view and prove that the
MPC request is invariant.

The current aggregate forecast must first be projected to a coherent deterministic solar
trajectory by scaling the known clear-sky shape inside each band while preserving its
issued energy and zero output at night. A p10 band is a marginal summary, not a coherent
scenario; no implementation will be called stochastic MPC until causal scenario members
exist.

SciPy's HiGHS-backed `milp` interface exposes solver success, dual bound, node count, and
MIP gap and will be evaluated for the commitment model
([SciPy `milp`](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.optimize.milp.html)).
Missing or unsuccessful solver execution may never silently fall back to a heuristic under
an MPC label.

## Development reporting

Parts I and II will initially run only on the six already-inspected 35/42 kg/h seed cells.
For every cell the report must retain:

- source start/end audit and dependency-lock hash;
- full/exogenous manifest identity;
- privileged-information label;
- solver/model or search/action-library identity;
- success/failure state and bounded diagnostic;
- A0, feasible-planner, unconstrained relaxed, and endowment-preserving values;
- exact planning gain `F - A0`, residual relaxed gap `U - F`, and fraction closed
  `(F - A0) / (U - A0)` only when `U - A0` exceeds the frozen numerical tolerance;
  the endowment-preserving diagnostic remains outside this bracket;
- complete initial and terminal balance vectors;
- deadline-level deliveries;
- starts/stops, process energy, curtailment, battery throughput, and execution latency;
- selected action schedule and digest for exact replay; and
- explicit separation of certified bounds from feasible outcomes.

Three development seeds support engineering decisions only. No mean, best episode, or
upper bound may be presented as field performance, financial return, RL superiority, or
evidence of site readiness.

## Required validation

1. Hand-solvable zero-resource, abundant-resource, one-step, battery, storage, and
   deadline-ordering LP fixtures, including full-tank refusal and exact within-minute
   headroom.
2. Sparse-model dimensional and finite-value validation before solver entry.
3. Independent recomputation of every LP balance and bound after solver return.
4. Exact traces embedded in the relaxation and never exceeding its delivery bound,
   including startup/standby power with zero H2, full-tank refusal, same-minute
   production/methanation/settlement, requested charge/support overlap, sunrise and
   midnight boundaries, and an initial state exactly at reserve.
5. Clone isolation, deterministic planner output, incumbent non-regression, and exact
   fresh replay.
6. Fail-closed behavior for solver/search/replay/source failures.
7. No privileged planning object accepted by the causal evaluator.
8. Explicit `F <= U`, SciPy marginal-sign, inequality-multiplier projection,
   exact-rational box-Lagrangian, requested-mass summation, and outward-conversion
   fixtures.
9. Complete repository regression and an independent pre-outcome source audit.
