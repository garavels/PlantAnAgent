# Prospective endogenous-reserve tail challenge

Frozen before any controller outcome from this suite. Designed after inspecting the earlier ordinary-operation study and one constructed startup diagnostic; therefore a new challenge study, not a retrospectively discovered held-out population benchmark.

## Question and scope

Can the three existing direct PPO policies trade ordinary modeled contribution for more reliable delivery under disruptions, when each controller must first create and maintain its own reserves? No retraining, policy selection, reserve intervention, action shield, additional nighttime reward, horizon extension, or cloud compute. Seven days, 180 kg methane each six hours, original common bus, readiness physics, rewards and 149-input/seven-action interface remain unchanged.

## Registered cases

Use all 16 new seeds 43000000–43000015. Preserve each seed's original sampled battery charge, gas stocks, stochastic weather and causal ensemble forecast configuration/random stream. Disable original random component faults solely to isolate the prescribed intervention. Cross each seed with all six conditions and five controllers (daylight, storage, PPO-101, PPO-211, PPO-307): 480 full weeks, one attempt each.

* Control: no additional disruption, original weather.
* pv6/pv18/pv36: zero solar for 6/18/36 hours starting elapsed hour 48, day three at 06:00. Restore the same sampled weather afterward.
* meth24/meth48: physical methanator outage lasting 24/48 hours, both ending elapsed hour 86, day four at 20:00. This gives 62/38 hours of autonomous reserve management before failure. The next delivery is hour 90.

Fault timing is fixed exogenously, never triggered by inventory. Physical intervals are [onset,end); minute-state health and power reflect the preceding interval. The fault return first becomes visible at the next 15-minute decision after the boundary. Neither future disruption severity nor a repair countdown is exposed. Original forecasts respond to current observed solar using their unchanged causal generator; they do not announce the imposed repair time.

## Outcomes and interpretation fixed before evaluation

Report every-delivery success (all 28 deliveries), unmet kg, missed deliveries, modeled weekly contribution, opening/ending stocks, reserves at onset/return, and unmet kg before/during/after the event. For control the common bookkeeping anchor is hour 48, not an intervention. All-week outcomes retain pre-existing failures; event-period losses are also reported to avoid attributing earlier losses to the shock. Worst-quarter mean unmet kg is descriptive of these prescribed scenarios, not population CVaR.

Each of three fixed PPO policies is compared separately with daylight and storage: six primary contrasts. Pool the five stress conditions with equal weight, yielding 80 stress cells per controller but only 16 independent base seeds. Resample base seeds with all five conditions together, 2,000 fixed-seed bootstrap replicates. Use two-sided 99.1666667% percentile intervals (Bonferroni adjustment over six contrasts); small-sample intervals remain approximate. Never treat the 80 cells as independent.

The declared evidence bar for each contrast is at least eight rescued all-delivery stress cells, zero regressions, no higher mean unmet kg, and a strictly positive adjusted interval lower bound for paired all-delivery success. This practical bar is a declared research choice, not a safety certification. Ordinary-control contribution and delivery differences are reported alongside any tail advantage. Report all policies and all severities even when no policy clears the bar. Failure under an intervention is not necessarily avoidable: feasibility requires a successful comparator or explicit physical witness. No real-world event-frequency, optimality, universal safety, or commercial-profit claim follows from this artificial suite.

## Evidence and execution

Pin this protocol, new environment, runner, analysis, auditor, tests, frozen parent sources, all checkpoint weights/normalizers/metadata before running. The new auditor copies the original independent interval balances and only generalizes declared health schedules from DAC-only to explicit component targets. Tests cover real fault boundaries and deliberately corrupted health. Audit every physical minute and economic decomposition in every case. Retain actual input/action witnesses for all decisions, and complete physical traces for the first prescribed seed in every controller-condition panel. Verify identical underlying sources/openings by seed and identical physical forcing among controllers in each condition.

Three local CPU worker processes. Evaluation deadline 2026-09-17 22:53 UTC reserves time to complete the paper before the existing four-hour limit at 23:09:38 UTC. Persist each attempted case, including failures; no silent reruns or result-dependent extensions. Incomplete or failed physical/economic audits invalidate a complete-panel claim. Reporting/packaging helpers are not scientific decision code and may be refined after outcomes; frozen sources may not.
