# Europe map data

Derived from Natural Earth 1:110m Admin 0 countries, downloaded 2026-09-15.

Source: https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_110m_admin_0_countries.geojson
License: public domain — https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

Country polygons intersecting longitude -25..45°, latitude 34..72° are projected to an equirectangular SVG with standard parallel approximately 50°. Coordinates use a 720×608 viewBox. The SVG viewport clips polygons to the study bounds. Borders are illustrative and do not express a position on territorial status.
